# 🧠 Drunkenness and Its Effects on the Brain and Others

## 1. **Neurobiological Effects of Drunkenness**

Alcohol (ethanol) impacts several key neurotransmitters in the brain:

### 🔹 **GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid)**
- Alcohol **enhances GABA** activity.
- GABA is the primary **inhibitory neurotransmitter**.
- This results in **drowsiness, impaired coordination**, and **slowed reflexes**.

### 🔹 **Glutamate**
- Alcohol **inhibits glutamate**, the main **excitatory neurotransmitter**.
- This leads to **slowed brain activity**, **poor memory**, and **reduced cognition**.

### 🔹 **Dopamine**
- Alcohol **stimulates dopamine** release in the **reward center** of the brain.
- Causes feelings of **pleasure, euphoria**, and **reinforces addictive behavior**.

### 🔹 **Serotonin**
- Alters serotonin levels, which affects **mood, sleep**, and **impulse control**.

---

## 2. **Effects on Others**

### 🧍‍♂️ **Emotional and Relational Impact**
- **Anger and aggression** often increase, damaging relationships.
- Can lead to **verbal abuse, physical abuse**, and **domestic violence**.
- Promotes **neglect of responsibilities** (e.g., parenting, marriage, work).

### 🚗 **Public and Social Harm**
- **Drunk driving** endangers lives—leads to accidents, injuries, and deaths.
- **Crime and disorder** rise in areas with high alcohol misuse.
- Contributes to **financial stress**, **homelessness**, and **social instability**.
