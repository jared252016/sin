# Envy: Effects on the Brain and Others

## 🧠 Neurobiological Effects of Envy

Envy activates brain regions associated with pain and threat. Key neurotransmitters and structures involved include:

### 1. **Dopamine**
- **Function**: Involved in reward and pleasure.
- **Effect**: When we compare ourselves to others and feel inferior, dopamine **decreases**, leading to dissatisfaction and craving what others have.
- **Implication**: Drives obsessive thoughts about others' success, fueling more envy.

### 2. **Cortisol**
- **Function**: Stress hormone released during threat or anxiety.
- **Effect**: Envy triggers a **stress response**, elevating cortisol levels, especially in competitive or social media environments.
- **Result**: Increased anxiety, irritability, and health risks over time.

### 3. **Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC)**
- **Function**: Involved in error detection and emotional conflict.
- **Effect**: Activated when we perceive social inequality — essentially when we think, *“Why not me?”*

### 4. **Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex (vmPFC)**
- **Function**: Regulates emotional responses and moral judgments.
- **Effect**: May be **underactive** during envy, reducing empathy and increasing bitterness.

## 👥 Social and Relational Effects of Envy

1. **Breaks Down Trust**
   - Envy creates resentment and suspicion, damaging friendships, marriages, and teams.

2. **Encourages Sabotage or Passive-Aggression**
   - People may downplay others' success or seek to undermine them subtly.

3. **Breeds Division and Gossip**
   - Envy fuels comparison, leading to slander, factions, or cliques (Galatians 5:20).

4. **Suppresses Generosity and Gratitude**
   - Envy blinds people to their blessings and encourages a scarcity mindset.

> "A heart at peace gives life to the body, but envy rots the bones."  
> — *Proverbs 14:30 NIV*

## 🙏 Spiritual Reflection

Envy is a condition both of the heart and mind. It corrodes from within and spreads destruction outwardly. Scripture warns against it not just for moral reasons, but because it *harms our very being*—physically, emotionally, relationally, and spiritually.

Let us ask the Lord to renew our minds with gratitude and joy in others' blessings.

> "Rejoice with those who rejoice..."  
> — *Romans 12:15 NIV*
